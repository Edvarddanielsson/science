Two variations of Home, Notes and Modes

Both drop the m. A camera-width bar sits right under the Dynamic Island, icons only, with a soft pill on the current screen. Tap the microphone on Home to see the recording state. Recording and file import are previews: no audio is captured and nothing is uploaded.

Variation A

Settings in the bar

The bar holds Notes, Add file and Settings. The current mode sits just above the microphone as an icon and a name; tap it to change mode.

Variation B

Modes in the bar

The bar holds Notes, Add file and Modes. Settings moves to a small gear in the bottom-right corner of Home, out of the way of recording.
