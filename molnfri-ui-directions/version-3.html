Version 3: Home, Notes and Modes

One bar under the Dynamic Island with Notes, Search, Add file, Modes and Settings. The screens are live: tap the microphone, open the tags, change a tag, pick a mode. Recording and file import are previews only; no audio is captured and nothing is uploaded.

Sample notes and counts are made up. Changes stay inside each phone and reset when the page reloads.
