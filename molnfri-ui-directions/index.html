Five directions for Home, Notes and Modes

Every direction keeps the same sky: curved bands that radiate from the microphone, a pale champagne record button with an ivory mic, and quiet translucent surfaces. What changes is how you move around, how notes and tags are grouped, and how a mode is chosen.

The screens are live. Open the menus, filter by tag, search, drag a note onto a tag and turn the mode wheels. Recording and import are previews only: no audio is captured and nothing is uploaded. Switch to Group by Screen to compare all five Home, Notes or Modes screens side by side.

  • Sky bands centred on the mic
  • Champagne record button
  • Ivory microphone
  • Deep teal ink
DIRECTION 01

Quiet reveal

Home shows only the microphone. A lowercase m under the camera opens a small tray with Notes, Upload and Modes, then folds away again. Inside, tags sit in a light grid of circles and the mode turns on an open wheel.

DIRECTION 02

Slim toolbar

A slim bar under the camera stays put, so Notes, Record, Modes and Import are one tap from anywhere. The current place gets a soft white background. Search is always open on Notes, and Modes is a plain list that applies on tap.

Tactile

DIRECTION 04

Editorial

Typography does most of the work. A serif m opens a typeset index, tags read as one line with counts, and notes run as a compact chronology split by hairlines. Hardly anything sits in a box.

DIRECTION 05

Orbit

The background rings become the layout. On Home, Notes, Import and Modes orbit the microphone. On Notes, tags form a dock along the arc where you drop notes. Modes is a dial that turns the chosen mode to the top.
