Version 6: Home, Notes and Modes

The menu now holds only Notes, Modes and Settings, with Modes in the middle, so it can be a little larger. It still shrinks by 30% inside a section; on Notes, Tags and Search sit on either side of it. While recording, cancel and pause sit in the bottom corners, and tapping the mic again ends the recording. Recording is a preview only; no audio is captured.

Sample notes and counts are made up. Changes stay inside each phone and reset when the page reloads.
