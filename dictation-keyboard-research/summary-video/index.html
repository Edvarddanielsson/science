Talk, don't type.

In Swedish. With nothing leaving the phone.

People are swapping their phone
keyboards for their voice.
Apps like Wispr Flow turn rambling
speech into text that's ready to send.
Reviewers rank Wispr Flow first,
with Aqua Voice and Willow close behind.
Almost all of them send your voice to the cloud.
The habit comes from not editing.
Wispr says eighty-nine percent of messages
go out untouched, sent in half a second.
People stop proofreading and
start thinking out loud.
Can it run on the phone? Yes,
but not inside the keyboard.
iOS kills a keyboard at around seventy megabytes,
so the models live in the main app.
Stream the cleaned-up text,
and words appear in about half a second.
And Swedish is wide open.
Apple adds no Swedish punctuation,
and its new AI dictation is English only.
Klang's Pianissimo model already gets
about four and a half percent word error
rate in Swedish, right on the phone.
The plan: edit-free Swedish text,
on the device, with Pianissimo and Gemma.
Talk, don't type.
