PROPOSED UI — concepts for review, not current screenshots or verified prototypes.

Ochre dashed outline = new or changed element. Numbered ochre badges = changes listed in the note beside each row. The Show annotations switch hides them.

Rank numbers (#) refer to REVIEW.md (molnfri-ux-review-2026-10-21). Rows 1–3 are the minimum first batch (#1 #2 #3 #4 #6). Rows 4–6 are later batches. Row 7 shows complementing Apple frameworks; row 8 structural alternatives.

Drawn in the app's own Solar style (sky bands, sun microphone, cream reading surfaces, glass capsules). No status bars are drawn.
